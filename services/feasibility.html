Questions

Common questions

How many hours a year justify owning?

There is no single number. Below about 150 hours charter or a card usually wins; above 300 ownership often does; the middle depends on your routes, aircraft and tolerance for responsibility. The study answers it for your case.

Do you sell aircraft or charter?

No. We are paid only by you, so the recommendation is not tied to a sale.

What if the answer is "don't buy"?

Then you saved a great deal of money for $6,500. We will tell you which charter or card arrangement fits instead.

Can you include tax treatment?

We model the common structures and flag the issues. Final tax advice comes from your CPA or aviation tax attorney, and we coordinate with them.
