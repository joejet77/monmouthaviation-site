Questions

Common questions

Is this for owners or operators?

Both. Owners use it to plan; small Part 135 operators use it to set charter rates that actually cover costs.

Where do the numbers come from?

Manufacturer and program data, your own invoices where available, current fuel and insurance quotes, and our operating experience. Every line shows its source.

Will you tell me if I am overpaying a management company?

Yes. The budget makes management fees and markups visible next to the market.
